### Hi, I'm Hanif

PhD student in NLP at **MBZUAI**, advised by Prof. Fajri Koto and Prof. Iryna Gurevych.

I work on **AI agency**: what models do when instructions are underspecified, model laziness, robustness, and long-horizon alignment.

> *"The question is no longer just what AI agents can do. It's what they will do when we haven't anticipated the path they take."* — Dawn Song

- 🎓 Ph.D. @ MBZUAI · B.Sc. Computer Science @ Universitas Indonesia (cum laude)
- 🌏 Research stints at SUTD (Singapore), JAIST (Japan), and MBZUAI (UAE)
- 🏆 3rd at BEA 2025 Shared Task (ACL) · 1st at Gemastik XVI, Data Mining
